4. The service

VeritasVPN provides a WireGuard VPN to servers in Paraguay, account management, and the clients listed above. Windows and macOS apps are not offered. A connection requires an active paid period, or an active Google Play free trial. An account with no active period cannot connect. Premium, including a Play trial, can have up to 5 devices connected at once.

Premium includes Veritas Shield on Android and Linux. Shield is a DNS filter for WireGuard clients that use the in-tunnel resolver. It has four toggles: malicious sites (malware, phishing, scam, and cryptomining), ads, adult domains, and trackers. The default is malicious sites and trackers on, and ads and adult domains off. A blocked name gets an NXDOMAIN answer. Shield does not remove ads or trackers that are already inside a page. Android and Linux can also use a Stealth transport. Stealth does not create a separate connection log.

The connection map in the Android and Linux apps shows the server in Asunción, Paraguay. The Android app does not request a location permission, and the map does not use your device location.
