Install securely

  1. Download the signed APK

    Tap the download button above on your Android device.

  2. Allow this installation

    If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source. You can turn the permission off again immediately afterward.

  3. Sign in and connect

    Open VeritasVPN, use your existing account, approve the Android VPN permission, enable Always-on VPN plus Block connections without VPN for VeritasVPN when prompted, and tap Connect now. DNS lookups go through Veritas Shield while the tunnel is up.

Protection: While connected, traffic uses a full-device WireGuard tunnel. The kill switch and auto-reconnect are always on (no in-app off options). IPv6 is routed into the IPv4-only tunnel and blocked rather than allowed to bypass it.
