Install from Google Play

  1. Get it on Google Play

    Open the Play listing above and install VeritasVPN. Play keeps the app updated. Premium on a Play install is billed through Google Play.

  2. Open the app

    Sign in or create an account. Anonymous Account ID sign-up works while Always-on lockdown is on. Email sign-up shows Verify your email, with Open email app and a resend cooldown, before you can sign in.

  3. Connect

    Tap Connect now. Approve the Android VPN connection if asked so VeritasVPN appears in system VPN settings. Then enable Always-on VPN and Block connections without VPN for VeritasVPN — the tunnel stays blocked until both are on. DNS lookups go through Veritas Shield while the tunnel is up.

Protection: While connected, traffic uses a full-device WireGuard tunnel. Connect requires Android Always-on VPN and Block connections without VPN (no in-app off option). Always-on steps. Auto-reconnect is always on. IPv6 is routed into the IPv4-only tunnel and blocked rather than allowed to bypass it.

Compatibility: Android 8.0 or newer on 64-bit devices. Devices that cannot use Google Play can use the signed APK linked above.

Connection modes

On restrictive networks that block or throttle plain WireGuard UDP, open connection settings (Settings → Connection) and choose a mode. Auto (the default) tries UDP WireGuard first, then Stealth if that handshake does not complete. Stealth wraps the tunnel in a TLS WebSocket on port 443 so traffic looks more like ordinary HTTPS. The VPN stays on during that switch. UDP only stays on plain WireGuard, with no Stealth fallback. Stealth always starts on Stealth. Reconnect to apply a change. This helps on harsh networks; it is not a claim of undetectability. Support steps.
