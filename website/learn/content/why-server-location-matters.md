## Latency and routes

Physics still applies. A distant exit adds round trips. Pick locations near you for everyday browsing when privacy allows.

## Geolocation of content

Streaming and news sites often key off the **exit IP’s geography**. That is a product feature for some users and a false sense of “being in another country” for others—you are still you, with a different network address.

## Legal and operational reality

Servers live in data centers under local law. Company HQ may differ from server country. Neither fact alone proves safety; both belong in due diligence. See [how to choose a VPN](/learn/how-to-choose-a-vpn.html).
