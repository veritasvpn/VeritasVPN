Cookie Policy

Effective: 2026-07-28 · Last updated: 2026-07-28

Short summary of how we use cookies and similar storage on veritasvpn.cloud. See also our Privacy Policy.

1. What we use

  • Essential session storage: tokens or flags needed to keep you signed in on the account pages and clients that talk to our API.
  • Preference storage: simple UI preferences (for example theme or dismissed notices) if enabled in the product.
  • Security / edge: Cloudflare or our reverse proxy may set cookies required for bot protection or routing.

2. What we do not use (today)

  • Third-party advertising cookies
  • Cross-site marketing pixels on the marketing homepage
  • Analytics SDKs that sell profiles to advertisers

3. Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out of the account portal. Desktop and extension clients store credentials locally according to each platform’s APIs; uninstalling removes most local data.

Questions: contact@veritasvpn.cloud · Contact.
