Delete your VeritasVPN account

You can ask us to delete your account from this page, from the apps, or by email. You do not have to be signed in to read these steps.

How to delete

  • Android app: open Account, then Delete account, then confirm. An email account confirms with the password. An Account ID account confirms with the security check.
  • Linux app: Account, then Delete account, then confirm.
  • Website: sign in, open Account, then Delete my account. Email accounts confirm with the password. Account ID accounts confirm with the security check.
  • Email: write to contact@veritasvpn.cloud and include the Account ID. Account ID accounts have no email on file, so the message has to include that ID. We delete what we control.

What is deleted

In the live database we delete the account, sign-in sessions, VPN peers, the subscription row, and payment records. You are signed out. This cannot be undone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted database backups until they expire: 7 daily, 4 weekly, and 3 monthly copies. Monthly copies are removed after about 93 days (about 3 months). Encrypted copies in Cloudflare R2 are removed after 60 days. We do not restore a deleted account into the live service except to recover from an incident.
  • BTCPay invoices tagged with the Account ID stay on the self-hosted BTCPay server. Account deletion does not delete them. The Google Play app does not use BTCPay.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Google can keep charging you until you cancel it in Google Play.
