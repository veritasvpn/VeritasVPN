IP check

Am I leaking my IP?

Every site you visit can see a public IP for this connection. That address is often enough to guess your region and tie sessions together.

What this checks

The public IP our edge sees for this browser request, plus coarse network hints (country, ASN, city when available) and an approximate map. It is not a street address.

Why it matters

Without a VPN, that IP is usually one your ISP or carrier assigned to this connection. Sites can use it to place you roughly (city-level) and correlate sessions; only the ISP can map it to a subscriber account. A tunnel makes destinations see the VPN exit instead.
