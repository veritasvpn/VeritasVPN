Breach check

Is my email in a data breach?

Stolen databases often include emails and passwords. Knowing an address already appears in a public dump helps you act before someone else does.

What this checks

Whether the email you enter appears in public breach corpora (via XposedOrNot). We proxy the lookup and do not store the address as a profile. Results can be incomplete.

Why it matters

A hit usually means that address — and often a password used with it — was exposed somewhere. Change reused passwords, enable 2FA, and treat a “clean” result as incomplete, not a full security audit.

Check an email

Prefer an address you are comfortable typing. Anonymous Veritas accounts do not require email.

Ready

    Breaches happen. Network exposure is separate.

    Even with a clean inbox history, your ISP can still see DNS and your IP on an unprotected path. VeritasVPN protects the connection; use unique passwords and a password manager too.
